NextRisk AI Dynamic Algorithmic Scoring Blueprint

Live Risk Score = [ (Asset Criticality × 0.35) + (CVSS v3 Base × 0.35) + (Threat Likelihood × 0.20) ] × (1 - Control Effectiveness × 0.70)

The core engine recalculates live risk scores whenever an asset tier, linked vulnerability, or compensating control is updated. The score is normalized from 1 to 100 and projected into financial exposure using the FAIR (Factor Analysis of Information Risk) model: ALE = SLE × ARO ($ USD).
